Copy this file, rename it (the file name becomes the URL: `_essays/on-chaos.md` → `/essays/on-chaos/`), and write in Markdown.

Paragraphs are separated by a blank line. *Italics*, **bold**, [links](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19605).

Inline math uses double dollar signs: $$f(x) = x^2$$. Display math goes in its own paragraph:

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}
$$
