Currently building

Dondarrion

Synthetic AI polling for the exact population you want.

Define a population (a single ward, renters under 35, people who voted a certain way last time, or even the intersection of that) and put a question to it. Dondarrion returns an estimate with its base, interval and Census frame, with no fieldwork. It can also test campaigns, ads and headlines on that population, side by side.

  • Seconds instead of the six weeks a field poll takes
  • Any group even ones phone polls can't reach
  • Census-anchored bias-corrected MRP

Visit dondarrion.com → Private beta for governments, public agencies and research institutions.
