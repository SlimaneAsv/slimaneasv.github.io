Currently building

Dondarrion

Precise and fast synthetic AI polling for the exact population you want.

Define a population (a single ward, renters under 35, people who voted a certain way last time) and put a question to it. Dondarrion returns an estimate with its base, interval and Census frame, with no fieldwork. It can also test campaigns, ads and headlines on that population, side by side.

  • Seconds instead of the six weeks a field poll takes
  • Any group even ones phone polls can't reach
  • Census-anchored bias-corrected MRP

Visit dondarrion.com → Private beta for governments, public agencies and research institutions.

On paper my work looks scattered: dynamical systems, cancer research, legal AI benchmarking, imaging, cognitive science, machine learning. To me it is one question asked in different places: how do objects interact, and what comes out of those interactions? A sequence pushing against a threshold that moves with it, cells signalling to their neighbours in a tissue, regions of a brain folded into an unusual shape. The objects change; the question doesn't.
